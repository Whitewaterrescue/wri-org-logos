Logo Prep

Everything stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

1Add the logo image

Drop an image here or paste one with Ctrl + V (right-click a logo on a website and choose “Copy image”) PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG. The biggest, cleanest version you can find.

2Keep the whole logo, or just the emblem

Empty margin is trimmed automatically. If the logo is a long wordmark, drag a box around the emblem and the box snaps to the artwork inside it.
